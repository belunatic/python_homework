Reflect on why websites use robots.txt and write 2-3 sentences explaining its purpose and how it promotes ethical scraping?
- robots.txt is a set of rules that tells automated crawlers which parts of a website they may or may not crawl. It helps websites manage server resources and discourages scraping of areas such as login pages, admin pages, and private content. By following these guidelines, scrapers can access information responsibly without overloading servers or collecting content from restricted areas.
